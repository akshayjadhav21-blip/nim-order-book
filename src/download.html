New India Motors · Order Book

Employee Android app

⬇ Download the app (APK)

First time on this phone

  1. Tap Download the app. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installs from Chrome – turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open NIM Order Book, sign in with the shop account (ask the owner), then log in with your name and PIN.

Updating

When the app shows New version available, tap it, download and install. It installs over the old one – your login stays.

Office computer

Open the office desktop app in Chrome or Edge and pin it to the taskbar.
